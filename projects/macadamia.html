Macadamia-shell filament

3D-printing filament made from macadamia shell waste, used on Doi Tung's hotel key fobs.

Chulalongkorn University · May 2024 – Mar 2025, Jun 2026 – present

The client

The Doi Tung Development Project is the royal initiative of the Mae Fah Luang Foundation under Royal Patronage. It turned opium-growing hillsides in northern Thailand into coffee and macadamia farms, and its processing leaves a steady stream of macadamia shell waste.

What I did

Working with a postdoctoral researcher and professor at Chulalongkorn University, I developed a 3D-printing filament that uses that shell waste as filler. I optimized the ratio of shell powder to PLA, compounded the mix into pellets, and drew it into filament on a twin-screw extruder line. I checked particle dispersion under SEM, then tested the filament by printing calibration parts and tensile specimens.

Result

The DOITUNG lettering on the hotel's room key fobs is printed in the macadamia filament, made from Doi Tung's own shell waste.
