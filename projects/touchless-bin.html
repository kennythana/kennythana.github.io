Touchless waste bin

An ultrasonic sensor and servo that open the lid without touch.

EK 210 Intro to Engineering Design · May – Jun 2026 · with Guy Margalit and Mohammed Sunbul

What it does

An HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor spots a hand within 50 cm, and a 35 kg·cm waterproof servo swings the lid open, holds it for 5 seconds, then closes it.

My part

I owned the CAD model and the servo mount in Onshape, and tuned the distance threshold and debounce timing to stop false triggers. We chose the sensor, actuator and power source from a function–means chart, then ranked goals with a pairwise comparison: no-touch hygiene first, cost last.

Trigger distanceUnder 50 cm, average of 3 readings
Lid cycleOpen 5 s, then 3 s cooldown
ActuatorDS3235 digital servo, 35 kg·cm, waterproof
Power8× AA pack, about 42 h estimated at 60 mA idle
Bill of materials$105.27, against a $200 target
