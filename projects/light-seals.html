Restoring film cameras

Finding and fixing light leaks in a Nikon F2 and a Mamiya RB67.

Personal maintenance

The problem

Film cameras keep stray light out with thin foam seals around the back door and film channels. After decades the foam breaks down into sticky crumbs, light gets in, and it fogs the film with streaks and blotches. I keep both cameras in working order so they are ready to shoot whenever I need them.

Finding the leaks

I ran a controlled test: I closed everything up, left the lens cap on, exposed blank frames in bright light and developed them. Any light on that film had to come through a gap rather than the lens, and where the fogging landed showed which seal had failed.

The fix

I scraped out the degraded foam, cleaned the channels of old adhesive, fitted precision pre-cut replacement seals, then shot full rolls to confirm the fix. It is the same loop I want to use on medical devices: reproduce the failure, isolate the cause, fix it and verify.
