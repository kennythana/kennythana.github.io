Prosthetic hand and splints

A body-powered e-NABLE hand, and Adapt3D splints printed for donation.

e-NABLE volunteer and 3D Printing Club, Northfield Mount Hermon · 2023–2024

e-NABLE Phoenix v3 hand

A body-powered prosthetic hand: bending the wrist pulls tension lines that close the fingers. I printed every part and assembled the joints, elastic and tension lines, earning e-NABLE's Phoenix v3 fabrication and assembly badge. In 2024 I was named an e-NABLE Community Student Leader.

Splints for donation

Through my 3D Printing Club, I printed a set of Adapt3D splints for donation. They print flat as a honeycomb lattice, then a clinician heats and shapes them to the patient's arm, so one printer can make fitted splints without a cast room.
