Wearable ECG + PPG band

In progress: measuring pulse arrival time from the chest and fingertip.

Personal project · Fall 2026 · in progress

Why

My grandmother's stroke is why I care about monitoring. After a stroke, heart rhythm and blood pressure get checked constantly, and both leave signals at the skin.

How it works

This band records an electrical signal (ECG) and an optical one (PPG) at the same time. The delay between the heartbeat in the ECG and the pulse arriving in the PPG, the pulse arrival time, tracks how fast the pressure wave travels. That is one route to estimating blood pressure without a cuff.

Plan

The goal is a validated measurement, not a gadget. Heart rate from the band will be compared against a commercial watch using Bland–Altman agreement analysis.

HardwareArduino Uno · AD8232 single-lead ECG · MAX30102 red/IR PPG at about 200 Hz
SoftwareFirmware and a Python live-plot logger, written
AnalysisBand-pass filtering, R-peak and pulse-foot detection, pulse arrival time, HRV
NextBreadboard build, rest and post-exercise sessions, agreement against the watch
