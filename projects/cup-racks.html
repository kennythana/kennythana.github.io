Dining hall cup racks

Organizers for the NMH dining hall, designed and batch-printed through my 3D Printing Club.

Northfield Mount Hermon · senior year, 2025

The problem

The dining hall stored its cups loose in brass bowls, so they toppled over and piled up. I offered to fix it.

What I did

I measured the cups with calipers and designed a holder in CAD that grips their tapered shape. Then I printed the holders in batches and glued them onto cut acrylic bases to make racks. It ran as a 3D Printing Club initiative with the NMH makerspace.

Result

The racks stayed in daily use through my graduation. I handed the CAD files to Jamie Rourke, who runs the makerspace, so the makerspace can print more.
