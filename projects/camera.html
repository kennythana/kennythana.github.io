6×17 panoramic camera

A 3D-printed panoramic film camera built around a large-format lens.

Personal build · 2026

What a 6×17 frame is

A film camera has no sensor. Light from the lens falls on film coated with light-sensitive silver halide crystals, and the picture appears when the film is developed. The larger the piece of film, the more detail each picture holds.

A 6×17 camera exposes a single frame 6 cm tall and 17 cm wide on 120 roll film, about 12 times the area of a 35mm frame. A roll gives just four pictures.

The build

Commercial 6×17 cameras are specialist gear. Mine is 3D-printed: I started from an open-source body and redesigned the lens cone around a Schneider Super Angulon 90mm f/8, a large-format lens whose image circle is wide enough to cover a 17 cm frame.

The hard part is precision. The lens has to sit at exactly the right distance from the film for the whole width to be sharp, and the body has to be completely light-tight. I iterated through several print revisions, measuring and adjusting the flange distance each time, until the frames came back sharp edge to edge.
