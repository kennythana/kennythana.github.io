Gold nanorods

Nanoparticles tuned to 1030 nm for photoacoustic stimulation of the retina.

Yang Lab, Boston University · Oct 2025 – present

The goal

The lab is developing photoacoustic retinal neuromodulation toward an eyedrop treatment for retinitis pigmentosa and age-related macular degeneration. Gold nanorods absorb near-infrared light and turn it into a tiny acoustic pulse that can stimulate retinal cells.

What I do

I synthesize and surface-modify gold nanorods (PEGylation, chitosan coating), tuning their plasmon resonance near 1030 nm. I characterize them with UV-Vis spectroscopy and oscilloscope-based photoacoustic measurement, and log batch conditions to make synthesis more reproducible.

Pictured: the UV-Vis spectrum of one batch. The longitudinal peak lands at 1041 nm, 11 nm from the 1030 nm target.
