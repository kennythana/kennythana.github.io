EZGrip

Handles that let my grandmother open cabinets again after her stroke.

Personal project · 2024

The problem

After her stroke, my grandmother's grip strength dropped. The flush cabinet doors and handle-less drawers at home left almost no ledge for her fingers, so she couldn't open them.

What I did

I designed attachments that give her fingers something to pull on, printed them, and fitted them around the house.

Result

I published three designs, a cabinet handle, a claw handle and a bar handle, as free files on Printables, where they have been downloaded about 40 times.
