Cassette deck restoration

Rebuilt the tape transport of a 1990s AIWA deck that would no longer play.

Personal repair · 2026

The problem

An AIWA AD-S37, a 1990s deck with Dolby B/C and HX Pro, would no longer play. Its rubber drive belts had degraded, melted and broken.

The repair

I took apart the tape transport, removed the capstan motor and flywheel, and cleaned the melted rubber off the pulleys and capstan. Then I routed new belts along the motor, flywheel and reel path and put it all back together. It plays again.
