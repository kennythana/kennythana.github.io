Room-temperature monitor

An Arduino monitor that warns when my film storage gets too warm or cold.

EK 131 · Boston University · solo build

Why

I built this to watch the temperature where I store my film.

How it works

A TMP36 sensor feeds an Arduino Uno, which shows °C and °F on an I2C LCD. If the room leaves 70–80 °F, a red LED flashes and a buzzer sounds. It runs from a 9 V battery in a holder I designed in Onshape and 3D-printed, or from USB.

I measured every part with calipers before laying out the box, prototyped on a breadboard, then soldered 22 AWG wire with heat shrink. I sized the LED resistors with Kirchhoff's voltage law, giving the red alarm LED the smaller resistor so it is brighter.

SensorTMP36, −40 to 125 °C
AlarmOutside 70–80 °F: red LED and 1 kHz buzzer
LED currentRed 13.6 mA (220 Ω) · green 3 mA (1 kΩ)
Power9 V battery, 80 mA draw, about 6 h
